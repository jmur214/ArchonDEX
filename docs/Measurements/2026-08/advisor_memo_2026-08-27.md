## The liquidity flag (stated before any optimisation)

**Money earmarked for tuition and living costs during school must NOT sit in the 40-year maximum-terminal-wealth machine.** That machine is built to ride drawdowns for decades; a bill that arrives on a fixed date is the one thing it is structurally unsuited to fund. This is a constraint, not a preference, and it outranks every optimisation below.

## Phase 1 — NOW (pre-school)

- **Roth: already maxing (~$7,000/yr).** Captured — there is no move here, which is the correct outcome to report.
- **401(k): none today**; eligibility possible in ~2 years at the current employer, and **no match exists to capture now**. That makes it a *calendar trigger to revisit at eligibility*, not an available move.
- **HSA: eligible in principle — the top *current* wrapper item, conditional on one fact.** If the health plan is HDHP-qualified, an HSA is the only triple-tax-advantaged wrapper available (deductible in, compounds untaxed, qualified withdrawals untaxed) and is strongest when contributions are **invested rather than spent**. **Verify HDHP status first** — if the plan does not qualify, this item disappears entirely.

## Phase 2 — DURING SCHOOL (~3 years)

- **Contribution capacity likely falls to ~zero.** The additional capacity below ($5,000/yr) is realistically a **pre- and post-school** figure, not a during-school one. Treating it as continuous would overstate the machine's runway by three years of contributions.
- **Low-bracket years are themselves an asset.** Two mechanisms only available while income is low: **tax-gain harvesting** (realising long-term gains at a 0% bracket to reset basis upward) and **low-bracket Roth conversions**. Both are certain-sign and both disappear when income recovers. That is a fact about the tax code and the calendar, stated so it is known — not a prompt to act on any particular timeline.
- **The liquidity constraint above binds hardest here.**

## Phase 3 — POST-SCHOOL

- **A 401(k) with a match almost certainly appears.** An unclaimed match is the single highest-certainty item in this entire memo whenever it exists — it is an immediate return, not a forecast. It does not exist today.
