1. **0/11 edges with t>2 and heavy redundancy (5 momentum, 6 value/quality) is an edge-compression problem, not a discovery problem.** Daniel-Hirshleifer-Sun (2020, *RFS* 33(4):1673–1736) collapse the anomaly zoo into just 3 factors: market plus a long-horizon financing factor (FIN, exploiting managers’ equity issue/repurchase decisions) plus a short-horizon earnings-surprise factor (PEAD); this “three-factor risk-and-behavioral model outperforms other proposed models in explaining a broad range of return anomalies.” Anton-Polk (2014, *JF* 69) show crowding/connectedness is a measurable, priced risk. The implication: compress to a handful of orthogonal sleeves rather than adding correlated variants that raise the multiple-testing bar without adding independent information.
1. **HRP (Lopez de Prado 2016, *JPM* 42:59) and HAR-RV (Corsi 2009) are the two best-documented, retail-implementable risk upgrades.** HRP delivers lower out-of-sample variance than the critical-line/Markowitz optimizer in Monte Carlo tests without needing matrix invertibility. HAR-RV cuts volatility-forecast error ~35–40% vs GARCH(1,1) out-of-sample, while LSTM adds only a non-significant 4–6%  — strong evidence that simple linear RV models dominate complex ML for vol forecasting.
1. **Intraday market-momentum is the best-documented intraday alpha that survives realistic costs at SPY scale.** Gao-Han-Li-Zhou (2018, *JFE* 129(2):394–414, SPY 1993–2013) show the first half-hour SPY return predicts the last half-hour return: the timing strategy has a Sharpe ratio of 1.08 (std 6.19%) versus a “much lower Sharpe ratio of 0.29” for buy-and-hold, with certainty-equivalent gains of 6.02% per annum (risk aversion 5). The combined-predictor out-of-sample R² rises to 3.3% on high-volatility days and to 6.9% in the 2008 crisis; the gross Sharpe is claimed robust to transaction costs. Zarattini-Aziz-Barbon (2024, Concretum/SFI 24-97) report a net-of-cost Sharpe of 1.33 (1985% total return 2007–2024)  but with a low ~37–43% win rate and optimistic cost assumptions vs live (an independent QuantConnect replication at leverage 1 got ≈0.40).
1. **Information theory and causal discovery are gates, not generators.** Mutual information (Kraskov-Stoegbauer-Grassberger estimator),  transfer entropy (Schreiber 2000),  and Friedman’s H-statistic (Friedman-Popescu 2008)  can diagnose whether N edges have exploitable non-linear interaction structure before you spend trials on a GBM metalearner. Lopez de Prado’s *Causal Factor Investing* (2023, Cambridge) argues most factor claims are spurious because they are associational, not causal — the PC/FCI algorithms (causal-learn, tigramite/PCMCI+) provide a falsification gate. 
1. **The “built but unused” anti-pattern is documented in Sculley et al. (2015, NeurIPS) “Hidden Technical Debt in Machine Learning Systems”**  — dead experimental code paths,  glue code, configuration debt, and “undeclared consumers.” Engine B at 1.9K LOC being “dead-knob” risk infrastructure is a textbook case. The remedy is a capability ledger + “definition of done” that includes wired-trained-run-and-measured, not just merged.

### Q1 — Autonomous discovery that produces surviving edges

**Verdict: LOW priority at single-developer retail scale; high risk of net-negative EV.**

**Sources:** Lopez de Prado, *Advances in Financial Machine Learning* (2018, esp. backtest overfitting, deflated Sharpe, combinatorial purged CV); Harvey-Liu-Zhu “…and the Cross-Section of Expected Returns” and Harvey-Liu “Backtesting” (deflated Sharpe / multiple-testing haircut); Bailey-Lopez de Prado “Deflated Sharpe Ratio”; MAP-Elites canonical work (Mouret-Clune 2015; Cully-Demiris 2018; Pierrot et al. 2022 MOME for per-cell Pareto fronts). AQR/Robeco practitioner framing: factor “deep history” out-of-sample validation, and the discipline of demanding an economic mechanism before a backtest.

**Expected lift:** Realistically **negative-to-neutral** in expectation unless paired with a hard economic-prior gate. The math: with ~270 honest accumulated trials, a deflated Sharpe gate already requires observed Sharpe materially above ~1.0 to clear. Autonomous search over a broad gene space generates thousands of implicit trials; the multiple-testing bar rises with the maximum-over-trials, so the *expected maximum* Sharpe under the null grows roughly with √(2 ln N). At N=10^4 trials the null max Sharpe can exceed ~1.3 over a decade of daily data — meaning naive QD search will manufacture “edges” that are pure overfit.

**Implementation specifics:**

- **If pursued at all**, constrain to MAP-Elites with (a) a *small* gene space encoding only economically-motivated primitives (no free-form operator soup), (b) behavioral descriptors = {holding period, factor-exposure profile, regime-conditionality}, (c) fitness = deflated Sharpe net of costs, NOT raw Sharpe, (d) per-cell Pareto front (MOME) on {deflated Sharpe, factor-orthogonality, turnover}.
- Libraries: `qdpy` or `pyribs` for MAP-Elites; `deap` for GP primitives.
- Population: meaningless below ~50/cell; grid of ~10–20 descriptor cells.
- **Failure modes:** (1) trial-count explosion silently breaking your deflated-Sharpe accounting — you MUST log every evaluated genome as a trial; (2) descriptor leakage where the “diversity” axis correlates with the fitness; (3) survivorship in the elite archive.
- **Minimum scale to be non-trivial:** you need the discovery to evaluate on a universe and history large enough that the *honest* deflated-Sharpe threshold is clearable — practically, a cross-section of dozens of instruments, not a handful, and a trial ledger that the MBL Gate-0 / deflated-Sharpe gate reads from directly.

### Q3 — Cross-asset systematic alpha on free data

### Q4 — Information-theoretic feature analysis for compound alpha

**Verdict: MEDIUM priority as a DIAGNOSTIC GATE before building a metalearner.**

**Sources:** Kraskov-Stoegbauer-Grassberger (2004, *Phys Rev E* 69:066138) kNN MI estimator;  Schreiber (2000, *PRL* 85:461) transfer entropy;  Friedman-Popescu (2008, *Ann. Appl. Stat.* 2:916) H-statistic; Greenwell-Boehmke-McCarthy (2018) PD-based interaction measure. 

**What it answers:** Before training a gradient-boosted metalearner to combine your 11 edges, measure whether non-linear interaction structure even exists. If pairwise/conditional MI between edge signals and forward returns is at noise floor, and Friedman’s H is ~0, a GBM cannot extract a compound edge that linear combination misses — combination is hopeless and you save the trials.

**Thresholds:** H-statistic near 0 = no interaction;  meaningful interaction typically H > ~0.1 with bootstrap CI excluding 0. For MI, compare against a shuffled-null distribution (permutation test); require MI exceeding the 95th percentile of the null. **Below the null threshold, non-linear combination is hopeless.**

**Implementation:**

- MI/CMI: `scikit-learn` `mutual_info_regression` (KSG-based) or `infomeasure`/`pyinform`; use k=3–5 neighbors for KSG.
- Transfer entropy: `PyIF` (KSG-based, fast)  or `pyinform.transfer_entropy(k=...)`; or `IDTxl` for multivariate. 
- H-statistic: `sklearn-gbmi` (Friedman-Popescu H on sklearn GBMs)  or the PD-based `vip`/`pdpbox` route in Python.
- **Failure modes:** (1) MI/TE estimators are badly biased at small N and high dimension — always benchmark against shuffled nulls; (2) Friedman-Popescu warn that collinearity among inputs manufactures *spurious* interactions, so de-correlate edges first  (directly relevant given your 5 momentum + 6 value variants); (3) financial series autocorrelation inflates TE — use block-bootstrap nulls consistent with your existing CI machinery.

### Q5 — Causal discovery for edge validation

**Verdict: MEDIUM priority as a falsification gate; HIGH conceptual value, modest implementation cost.**

**Sources:** Pearl *Causality*; Lopez de Prado *Causal Factor Investing* (2023, Cambridge Elements) and Lopez de Prado-Zoonekynd “Causality and Factor Investing: A Primer” (CFA Institute) + “Factor Mirage” (2025, SSRN 5277078) on confounder vs collider bias; Spirtes-Glymour-Scheines (PC/FCI);  Runge et al. (2023, *Nature Rev Earth Environ*) PCMCI/PCMCI+ for time series. 

**Minimum viable implementation:** Wire a causal-discovery stage into the discovery gauntlet *after* an edge clears statistical significance but *before* it earns capital:

1. Build the candidate causal graph among {edge signal, known factors (FF5+Mom), macro confounders from FRED, forward return} using PC (`causal-learn`) for contemporaneous and PCMCI+ (`tigramite`) for lagged/time-series structure. 
1. Check whether the edge→return link survives conditioning on the factor set (confounder control) and that you are NOT conditioning on a collider (Lopez de Prado’s “factor mirage”).
1. Estimate the effect with `DoWhy` (model → identify → estimate → refute), running the placebo/random-common-cause refuters.

**Expected lift:** Does not raise Sharpe directly; it *lowers the false-discovery rate*, which is exactly your binding constraint given 0/11 survivors and a rising multiple-testing bar. A coincident-not-causal edge that fails the do-calculus check should be killed before consuming a backtest trial.

**Failure modes:** PC/FCI assume causal sufficiency / faithfulness; with hidden confounders use FCI  or LPCMCI. Conditional-independence tests are low-powered at retail sample sizes — use `ParCorr` for linear, `GPDC`/`CMIknn` for non-linear  but expect wide uncertainty. Treat the output as a Markov equivalence class (a CPDAG), not a unique graph.

### Q7 — HMM kill-switch as transition trigger

**Verdict: #1 PRIORITY. Highest EV, lowest trial consumption, machinery already validated.**

You have a validated 3-state Gaussian HMM (OOS AUC@5d 0.914, ci_low 0.880 on p_crisis+p_stressed, fires 7/7 stress events). The dwell-time problem (44–50% always-on, median run 12–19 days) correctly disqualifies it as a *level*. The literature on converting a level into a *transition trigger*:

**Sources:** Moreira-Muir (2017) and Harvey et al. (2018) for the vol-target mechanism; Ang-Bekaert (2002, *RFS* 15:1137) “International Asset Allocation With Regime Shifts” — regime-switching adds the most value when the investor can rotate to **cash** in the bear regime   (directly relevant: de-gross, don’t just rebalance equities); Ang-Bekaert (2004) “How Do Regimes Affect Asset Allocation?” — “When a persistent bear market hits, the investor switches primarily to cash”; Bekaert-Engstrom regime models; Tu (2010)/Stambaugh on time-varying expected returns and the cost of ignoring regimes. **Critical caveat:** Cederburg et al. (2020) / Liu-Tang-Zhou (2019) — naive vol-scaling has look-ahead bias; the real-time-implementable version is conditional de-grossing.

**Canonical transition-trigger design:**

- Trade the *change* in posterior, not the level: trigger de-gross when Δ(p_crisis+p_stressed) over k days exceeds a threshold, OR on the first crossing into the stressed state after a benign run — not continuously while the state is “on.”
- Use it as a **gross-exposure multiplier** (e.g., 1.0 → 0.5 → 0.0) with hysteresis (different on/off thresholds) to avoid whipsaw given the long p90 dwell (198–265 days).
- Re-grossing should be slower than de-grossing (asymmetric, consistent with the leverage-effect momentum overlay in Harvey et al.).

**Expected lift:** This is where the bull-conditional 16-yr Sharpe 1.018 / 26-yr 0.246 gap gets closed. Capturing even part of the documented vol-timing benefit (Moreira-Muir’s ~25% market-Sharpe increase is the upper anchor) converts the system from “survives only in bull cells” toward regime-robust. Plausible: lift the full-sample Sharpe meaningfully and cut crisis drawdowns substantially, while consuming only a handful of trials (you are testing one mechanism with a few threshold/hysteresis parameters, not searching a space).

**Implementation:** `hmmlearn` (already in use); the overlay is `pandas`. **Failure mode:** parameter-mining the threshold/hysteresis — pre-register the grid and count it against trials; out-of-sample the trigger on the crisis-inclusive retrain you already validated.

### Q10 — The artisanal edge-proliferation problem

**Verdict: HIGH priority. Compress before you add.**

**Sources:** Daniel-Hirshleifer-Sun (2020, *RFS* 33(4):1673–1736) — 3-factor behavioral model (market + FIN + PEAD) explains a broad anomaly set, showing the zoo compresses to a few orthogonal dimensions; Anton-Polk (2014, *JF* 69) “Connected Stocks” — crowding/co-ownership is a measurable priced risk (compression should account for crowding-correlation, not just return-correlation); Harvey-Liu-Zhu multiple-testing; Green-Hand-Zhang on which characteristics are independently informative.

**Published methodology for compressing an edge inventory while preserving coverage:**

1. **Orthogonalize then cluster:** run the 11 edges through FF5+Mom HAC OLS (you already do this), keep the *residual* alpha streams, then hierarchically cluster on the residual-return correlation matrix (the HRP dendrogram does double duty here).
1. **Keep one representative per cluster** (highest cost-adjusted information ratio), or build a single combined signal per cluster — collapsing 5 momentum variants to 1–2 and 6 value/quality to 1–2.
1. **Re-test the compressed set’s joint factor alpha** — the hypothesis is that 3–4 orthogonal sleeves clear t>2 jointly even though 11 redundant ones did not, because redundant variants split the same signal and inflate the multiple-testing penalty without adding independent information.
1. **Account for crowding** (Anton-Polk): a compressed sleeve that is heavily crowded carries reversal risk; weight by capacity/crowding, not just historical Sharpe.

**Expected lift:** Compression doesn’t add raw return but it (a) can move the surviving signal across the t>2 bar by removing redundant trials, (b) shrinks N_trials growth, and (c) stabilizes the portfolio. This is plausibly the cheapest way to get a factor-significant edge out of existing material.

## SINGLE HIGHEST-EV EXPERIMENT (pre-registration framing)

**Experiment:** Regime-triggered gross-exposure overlay on the existing strongest-cell portfolio.

- **Hypothesis (H1):** Applying a de-grossing multiplier triggered by an upward transition in the HMM (p_crisis+p_stressed) combined posterior — with hysteresis — raises the full-26-year Sharpe from 0.246 toward the bull-cell level and reduces maximum drawdown by ≥25%, relative to the un-overlaid book, out-of-sample on the crisis-inclusive retrain.

- **Threshold to “win”:** Deflated Sharpe improvement with block-bootstrap ci_low > 0 on the *difference* in Sharpe, AND max-drawdown reduction ≥25%, AND the result holds across both crisis and non-crisis sub-samples (no single-event dependence).

- **Expected N consumption:** ~36 trials against the ~270 accumulated (raising N to ~306, ~13% increase). Because this tests ONE mechanism with a small pre-registered grid (not an open search), the deflated-Sharpe penalty is modest and clearable if the effect is real.
