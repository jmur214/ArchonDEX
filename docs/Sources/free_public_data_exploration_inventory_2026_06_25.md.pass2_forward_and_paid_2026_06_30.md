## The two worth opening

### A1 ⭐ — Forward-only LLM-on-NEWS/text → equity signal (the real shot, FREE)
- **Why it's the one genuine shot:** it's the ONE place where "can't be backtested" is a *source of possible inefficiency* (investor inattention to text — the Lazy-Prices mechanism — is a documented *economic* mechanism), not merely a measurement obstacle. Orthogonal to the H0-exhausted price vocabulary. Free (EDGAR text + a free news feed + pennies of LLM). `[NN-AI-GATE]`-clean because **forward-only is the only look-ahead-clean LLM design** — exactly what the paper machine was built for.
- **HONEST CAVEATS (load-bearing):** (1) our own **T-237 already tested the zero-LLM 10-K text-change (Lazy Prices) as a clean backtest → H0 ("just beta")** — so the *filing-text* modality is already closed; the genuinely-untested part is **LLM-on-real-time-NEWS**, a different modality. (2) Lopez-Lira/Tang's positive is concentrated in the *non-tradable initial reaction* + small stocks/negative news, and decays as adoption rises; Glasserman-Lin show look-ahead + distraction bias are real. (3) **Timeline to significance: 2–4+ years** (forward monthly signal, few independent obs/yr). Prior: **LOW-MODERATE**, tempered by T-237.
- **Validation design:** pre-register ONE frozen spec before the first live scoring; ≤3 specs total with a Bonferroni/DSR forward-CI haircut; charge every prompt/re-score to N_trials (`[NN-MBL]`); **run an anonymized-ticker control arm** (Glasserman-Lin) — if named vs anonymized diverge, you're measuring memorization, not signal.

Reopening beyond free-backtestable is **unlikely to find deployable alpha; the defensive sleeve remains the win.** The excluded space isn't a secret garden — it's excluded because it's un-backtestable (market hides inefficiency AND you can't prove edge) or doesn't fit a long-only daily Roth.
