# Fresh-Eyes Strategic Brief — adversarial outside review (2026-06-26)

**Source:** a skeptical, sourced research pass commissioned to CHALLENGE the project's framing, not confirm it. This is the condensed, actionable distillation. It does NOT overturn the price-machine H0 (robust — exhaustively tested in isolation, the most-arbitraged pond) but it REOPENS the broader question via real blind spots + methodology corrections.

## Executive verdict
"Deploy trend and stop" is too defeatist by exactly three things:
1. **The benchmark has a known flaw.** Schwab robo force-holds 6-10% (up to ~22.5%) cash at below-market yield — a **$187M SEC settlement**. Beating it on *drawdown* is trivial (hold less equity); on *return* it's partly a FREE win (just be fully invested). The honest bar = **return net of the robo's cash-drag, risk-adjusted** — don't reward "hold cash-like assets."
2. **Our "comprehensive H0" is comprehensive only over the LIQUID, SCALABLE universe** — the most-arbitraged pond, where a $5-15K account's structural edges (no capacity limit, agility, patience) buy nothing. The retail-*advantaged* universe (micro-cap, special situations, spin-offs) has real wider spreads *because institutions are excluded* — BUT it has no free survivorship-free data + ~3.8%/yr honest cost → **real-but-unreachable on our constraints.** So the H0 is a CONSTRAINT ARTIFACT, not "no alpha exists."
3. **We never tested the orthogonal, FREE, flow/calendar/conditional effects** — where "comprehensive" is actually a coverage gap.

## Methodology corrections (IMPORTANT — some H0s may be too harsh; apply going forward)
1. **Sortino/MaxDD = a CI-bounded SCORECARD, NEVER an optimization TARGET.** Optimizing *for* downside metrics selects overfit params with worse OOS decay (arXiv 2602.00080). Sortino's bootstrap CI is WIDER than Sharpe's (estimated from the few bad months) → `[NN-SHARPE-CI]` applies MORE strictly to Sortino, not less. MaxDD is a single-path order statistic (we burned ourselves: T-171). Never let the search select on raw Sortino/MDD.
2. **The MBL/DSR gate may be TOO HARSH (false-negative risk).** DSR assumes INDEPENDENT trials; our ~75 N_trials are mostly correlated price-vocabulary variations → **effective independent N is far lower → the bar is INFLATED → a real edge near the threshold gets wrongly REJECTED.** Cluster the trials; use EFFECTIVE-N. (DSR's own authors migrated to Bayesian FDR.) The price-machine H0 still stands; near-threshold edges deserve the corrected gate. (Jensen-Kelly-Pedersen JF 2023: finance does NOT have a replication crisis — 80%+ replicate.)
3. **Our cost model is a false-negative generator for LIQUID ETFs.** Commission-free + 1-2bps spreads + near-zero impact at our size. Institutional/small-cap costs are WRONG for liquid ETFs (correct for single small-caps → a reason to avoid that universe, not turnover).
4. **The Roth's ZERO tax-drag is a structural edge we under-weight.** The #1 reason TAA underperforms (cap-gains tax on turnover) doesn't apply; the robo's tax-loss-harvesting is worthless in a Roth. We can afford turnover + convex carry a taxable account couldn't.
5. **Isolation/unconditional testing = a false-negative generator.** Conditional-regime alpha tests flat unconditionally. Our **insider-cluster dead end may be MIS-SPECIFIED, not null** — PEAD vanishes after insider-purchase-preceded announcements; the *interaction* has edge (we tested clusters in isolation).

## Blunt overall verdict
Achievable on **drawdown/tail terms: yes** — basically already done (trend + gold + conditional vol-targeting = a better *defensive* portfolio). On **return terms net of everything: low prior**, and every honest improvement correctly shrank the edge — BUT not yet fully tested (the orthogonal free directions). **Honest path: deploy the trend/barbell defensive sleeve NOW (it works, on the right asymmetric objective) + spend the remaining research budget on the 2 cheap orthogonal probes (calendar/flow; EDGAR text) — NOT one more price-machine variation.
